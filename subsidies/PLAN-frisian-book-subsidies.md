## 1. Summary / recommendation

History books in Frisian qualify as **original Frisian non-fiction**, which is eligible under the
Province of Fryslân literature schemes (governed by section 2.6 of the *Subsidieregeling Kultuer
en Mienskip Fryslân*). The schemes are **cumulative** — a single title can draw on several of
them across its lifecycle. The recommended play is to **stack** them, apply **early in each
window** (the publishing pot regularly hits its ceiling), and confirm **De Fryske Wrâld's
statutory eligibility with Afûk** before the next publishing round opens (~Aug–Oct 2026).

---

## 2. Eligibility — De Fryske Wrâld

The publishing scheme is open to a foundation (*stichting*) **whose primary purpose is the
Frisian regional language**, or a publisher affiliated with a trade body.

- ✅ Likely eligible **if** the *statuten* (articles) name the **Friese taal** in the doelstelling.
- ⚠️ Risk: if the stated purpose is "history" generally with no Frisian-language anchor, an
  assessor may push back. Fix = minor statutory wording adjustment.
- **Action:** send the statuten to Afûk and get written confirmation the doelstelling clears the
  bar **before** applying. (Afûk: **058-292 59 25**.)

## 3. The schemes (stack these)

| # | Scheme | What it funds | Indicative amount | 2026 window (verify) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Writing** (Schrijven) | Authoring the original Frisian non-fiction text | grant per project | long window (~Jan–Oct) |
| 2 | **Translating & adapting** (Vertalen en bewerken) | Producing a Frisian edition from another language (or vice-versa) | ceiling €20,000 / opening | per opening |
| 3 | **Publishing** (Uitgeven) | Print + e-book + audiobook production | ~€2,000 / title | **2nd round ~Aug–Oct 2026** |
| 4 | **Promotion** (Promotie) | Launch / marketing (NOT the publishing cost itself) | max €3,000 / application | per opening |

Publishing scheme ceiling: **€75,000** total — known to hit "Plafond bereikt." **Apply early.**

> Only relevant if translating: scheme 2. A purely Frisian-original history book skips it.

---

## 4. Per-title playbook (lifecycle order)

1. **Before/during writing →** apply to **Writing** scheme for the authoring effort.
2. **(If translating) →** apply to **Translating & adapting**.
3. **Production ready →** apply to **Publishing**, early in the Aug–Oct 2026 window.
   Bundle **paper + e-book + audiobook** in one application (all qualify; audio suits history readerships).
4. **Book exists →** apply to **Promotion** for launch costs (separate €3,000 claim).

**Rule:** don't claim the same cost line twice. Structure each application around a *distinct*
cost (writing vs. production vs. promotion) to respect double-funding / de-minimis rules.

## 5. Beyond the province (co-funding)

- **Fryske Akademy** — scholarly history / heritage angle.
- **Leeuwarden City of Literature** — networks, visibility.
- **Nederlands Letterenfonds** — national, esp. for translation.
- These can **co-fund alongside** the provincial schemes rather than compete.
